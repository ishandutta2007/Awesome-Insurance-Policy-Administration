# Awesome-Insurance-Policy-Administration

## Top Insurance Policy Administration Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Policy Lifecycle, Quoting, Issuance, Endorsements, Renewals & Core Insurance Administration*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Insurance Policy Administration**. These systems manage the full policy lifecycle—quoting, rating, underwriting, issuance, endorsement, renewal, and cancellation—across personal and commercial lines.



**Examples** include Guidewire PolicyCenter, Duck Creek Policy, Sapiens, Majesco, Insurity, EIS Suite, Keylane, Socotra, OneShield, and BriteCore (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full policy administration systems (PAS) are almost entirely commercial. Practical open options include the **openinsuranceplatform** (with policy and claims capabilities), experimental policy management projects, and insurance CRM prototypes. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the enterprise gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Guidewire PolicyCenter](https://www.guidewire.com/)**  

  Dominant P&C policy administration system for large carriers covering quote, bind, issue, endorse, renew, and cancel across multi-line operations.



- **[Duck Creek Policy](https://www.duckcreek.com/)**  

  Cloud-native policy administration platform with low-code configuration for rating, coverage structures, and endorsement workflows.



- **[Sapiens CoreSuite Policy / Sapiens](https://www.sapiens.com/)**  

  Comprehensive core insurance platform delivering policy administration across lines of business and distribution models.



- **[Majesco Policy](https://www.majesco.com/)**  

  Policy administration capabilities within Majesco’s core suite for P&C and specialty insurance carriers.



- **[Insurity Policy Decisions](https://www.insurity.com/)**  

  Policy administration and decisioning platform for P&C insurers, supporting rating, underwriting, and policy lifecycle.



- **[EIS Suite](https://www.eisgroup.com/)**  

  Core insurance platform providing policy administration, product definition, and multi-line support for carriers.



- **[Keylane](https://www.keylane.com/)**  

  Policy administration and core systems focused on European and specialty insurance markets.



- **[Socotra](https://www.socotra.com/)**  

  API-first policy administration platform designed for modern and embedded insurance products with rapid configuration.



- **[OneShield Policy](https://www.oneshield.com/)**  

  Configurable policy administration system supporting product definition, rating, and policy lifecycle management.



- **[BriteCore](https://www.britecore.com/)**  

  Cloud P&C policy administration suite aimed at small to mid-sized insurers seeking an all-in-one configurable platform.



- **[Genasys Unify and other modern PAS offerings](https://www.example.com/)**  

  Additional cloud and API-oriented policy administration systems serving specialty and mid-market carriers.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Evaluating **openinsuranceplatform** for a comprehensive open core covering policy, claims, billing, and commission.

- Using **open_insure** for mutual or group self-insurance models with transparent claim settlement.

- Building specialty or embedded products with open product engines + workflow tools rather than full commercial PAS.

- Accepting that multi-line enterprise policy administration, mature rating engines, and long-term carrier support still favor commercial platforms (Guidewire, Duck Creek, Sapiens, Majesco, Insurity, EIS, Socotra, BriteCore, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on composability, API-first design, and lower implementation cost for MGAs and specialty carriers.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Define products and rates with open rule engines → manage policy lifecycle in openinsuranceplatform or prototypes → handle billing and claims with open modules → expose APIs for embedded distribution → present agent portals with open web stacks. Suitable for MGAs, specialty lines, and teams building modern insurance platforms. Most large carriers still rely on commercial PAS suites for scale and regulatory depth.
